What it is

Book Release Tracker keeps an eye on the audiobook series in your Audiobookshelf library and matches each one against Hardcover's catalog. When a new book in a series you already own shows up there, whether it's just been announced with a future date or it's already out, you get told about it instead of having to remember to go check.

Notifications go out through whichever channels you turn on: a log entry (always on), ntfy, and email. There's also a web dashboard for browsing what's upcoming, what's new, your watchlist, and any series it couldn't confidently match on its own.

It's built for people who follow long-running series and don't want to keep manually searching for release dates. Run it as a one-shot check from cron, or as a daemon that checks on a schedule and serves the dashboard continuously.

Features

  • Series matching: syncs your Audiobookshelf libraries and matches each series against Hardcover by normalized name, with a manual override for anything it can't resolve on its own
  • Release alerts: notifies you once when a book in a followed series is announced (upcoming) and once when it's actually released, never twice for the same state
  • Multiple notification channels: a log notifier that's always active, plus optional ntfy and email
  • Web dashboard: pages for recent check-run history, upcoming releases, new releases, your watchlist, and unmatched series, all filterable by series or author
  • Watchlist with custom reminders: watch a specific upcoming release and choose how many days before (or after) it comes out you want the reminder to fire
  • Partial checks: skip series that already have a future release date on file, so scheduled runs don't waste time re-checking things with nothing new to learn
  • Audnexus enrichment: optionally pulls narrator, runtime, and summary details for releases that have an ASIN, without ever blocking a notification if it fails
  • Browser-based settings: edit your config.toml straight from the dashboard, with validation on save and secrets that never display their stored value

Getting started

You'll need a Rust toolchain, an Audiobookshelf API key (from your ABS user's settings), and a Hardcover API token (from your Hardcover account settings). Copy the example config, fill in your Audiobookshelf and Hardcover details, then build and run.

cp config.example.toml config.toml

cargo build --release

book-release-tracker --config /path/to/config.toml check
book-release-tracker --config /path/to/config.toml daemon
